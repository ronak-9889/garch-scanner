GARCH Scanner

Scans eligible NSE/BSE equities across the discovered exchange universe and provides a separate global-index monitor. Choose Yahoo Finance by default, or configure Dhan for Nifty 50 benchmark candles.

Educational research only; not investment advice. Statistical ranges are estimates, not guaranteed highs/lows. This software never places orders.

Data provider settings



Credentials are saved in this computer's local local application configuration and sent to Dhan only. Dhan mode in this release applies to the Nifty 50 benchmark candles; the full NSE/BSE equity scanner and global indices still use Yahoo Finance. This app does not place orders.


The full-market scanner uses the discovered NSE/BSE equity universe, not only Nifty 50. The first full-market refresh can take a long time because thousands of symbols may be requested individually. Yahoo Finance is unofficial and may rate-limit requests; unsupported tickers are skipped.
