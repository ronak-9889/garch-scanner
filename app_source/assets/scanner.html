NSE + BSE equity scanner

The expected move for every supported NSE/BSE equity discovered from the exchange lists (tomorrow, next week or next month), from the same textbook GARCH(1,1) used on the chart, fitted one stock at a time. It says how far a stock can move, never which way. The wide fence is a nominal 95% range and the inner fence a nominal 68% range; actual coverage can differ.
