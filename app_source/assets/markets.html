Global range forecasts

GARCH volatility ranges for Taiwan, the Nasdaq Composite, the S&P 500 and South Korea's KOSPI. Each index uses its own historical daily returns to estimate a range for tomorrow, the next trading week and the next trading month. These are statistical ranges, not predictions of direction.

Forecast ranges are fitted from historical daily data using the same GARCH(1,1) approach as the Nifty chart and stock scanner. The inner band is approximately 68% and the wide band approximately 95% under the model assumptions; actual coverage can differ. Yahoo Finance is unofficial and best-effort, so unavailable or insufficient data is shown explicitly.

Educational market context only; not investment advice. The ranges estimate possible movement, not direction or guaranteed highs/lows. The latest completed close is used internally as the anchor for range levels, but this page focuses on forecast ranges rather than displaying the close as the main result. Dhan remains an optional provider for Indian benchmark data; Yahoo Finance supplies the global index histories.
